# Application Access and Account Unlock

## Account locked
Your account locks automatically after five failed sign-in attempts and unlocks after 30 minutes. To unlock it sooner, ask the helpdesk chatbot to unlock your account. Standard employee accounts can be unlocked automatically; privileged accounts need the Identity and Access team.

## Requesting access to an application
1. Identify the application name and the role you need (for example "read-only" or "editor").
2. Raise a service request in the self-service portal. Your manager is asked to approve the access.
3. Once approved, access is granted within one business day and you receive a notification.

## Cannot sign in to an application
1. Confirm you can sign in to other company systems. If not, your password may have expired.
2. Use the company single sign-on page and avoid saved browser passwords.
3. Clear the browser cache and cookies for the application, then try again.
4. If you receive "You do not have permission", request access as described above.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise an incident with category Application / Access if the application reports an error even though you have the right permissions.
