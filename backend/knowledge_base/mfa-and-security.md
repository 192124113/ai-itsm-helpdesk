# Multi-Factor Authentication and Security Basics

## Setting up multi-factor authentication (MFA)
1. Install the company authenticator app on your phone.
2. Visit the security settings page in the employee portal and choose Add Device.
3. Scan the QR code with the authenticator app and enter the six-digit code to confirm.
4. Save your backup codes in a safe place.

## Lost or replaced phone
Contact the helpdesk to reset your MFA registration. Identity verification by your manager is required before the reset. This action is never automated.

## Phishing and suspicious email
Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected messages. Use the Report Phishing button in Outlook, or forward the message to the security mailbox. If you already entered your password on a suspicious page, change your password immediately and raise a security incident.

## Locking your screen
Lock your laptop whenever you step away. Press the Windows key and L, or Control, Command and Q on a Mac.
