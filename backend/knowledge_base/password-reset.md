# Password Reset and Expiry

Corporate passwords expire every 90 days. You receive reminders 14, 7 and 1 day before expiry.

## Password has expired or was forgotten
1. Request a password reset through the helpdesk chatbot or the self-service portal.
2. A temporary password is sent to your registered mobile number or personal recovery email. It is valid for 24 hours.
3. Sign in with the temporary password and choose a new one when prompted.
4. Sign out of all devices (laptop, phone, mail client) and sign in again with the new password so old credentials are not retried.

## Password rules
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include upper case, lower case, a number and a symbol. You cannot reuse your last 10 passwords.

## Safe to automate
Password resets for standard employee accounts are approved for automation. Accounts flagged as privileged or administrator accounts must always be handled by the Identity and Access team.

## Still cannot sign in
If the account shows as locked, see the Account Unlock section of the Application Access article. Otherwise raise an incident with category Access / Password.
